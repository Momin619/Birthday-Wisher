# 🎂 Birthday Wisher

An interactive birthday celebration page — light the candles, blow them out, and reveal a personal wish!

🔗 **Live Site:** [birthday-wisher-lake.vercel.app](https://birthday-wisher-lake.vercel.app?name=Momin&age=18)

---

## What It Does

You share a link with someone's name and age in it. They open the link and get a beautiful, personal birthday page with:

- 🎂 An animated 3-tier birthday cake
- 🕯️ Glowing candles they can light and blow out
- 🎊 Confetti explosion after blowing the candles
- 🔔 Sound effects (no audio files — generated by the browser)
- 💌 A personalized birthday message that appears at the end

---

## How to Use It

The only way to open the birthday page is with a URL like this:

```
https://birthday-wisher-lake.vercel.app/?name=Sara&age=22
```

Just replace `Sara` with the person's name and `22` with their age.

If you open the site **without** these in the URL:

```
https://birthday-wisher-lake.vercel.app
```

It will show:

> *"Missing details — Open this page with a URL like: `?name=Tallulah&age=25`"*

---

## The Birthday Experience (Step by Step)

1. Open the link → their **name and age** appear on a beautiful animated page
2. Click **"Light Candles"** → candles glow one by one with a soft sound
3. A hint appears: *"Close your eyes. Make a wish."*
4. Click **"Blow"** → flames go out one by one with a puff of smoke
5. **Confetti bursts** 🎊 and a personal birthday message slides in
6. Click **"↺ Start a New Celebration"** to reset
